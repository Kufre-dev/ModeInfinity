Ready-Made and Custom Footwear Terms

These terms explain how ModeInfinity supplies ready-made footwear and produces made-to-order and bespoke commissions — including specification, measurements, approval, pricing, timing, delivery, defects, cancellation and remedies.

1. Application

1.1 This Agreement applies to ready-made footwear and any made-to-order or bespoke commission identified in the Order Schedule.

1.2 The Order Schedule, accepted quotation, approved specification, measurement record, documented changes, payment schedule and these Terms form the agreement.

1.3 Mandatory Nigerian consumer rights prevail. No provision removes a remedy or duty that the law protects.

2. Definitions

"Ready-made" means footwear offered from existing stock or a standard range. "Made-to-order" means a standard design produced after order. "Custom/bespoke" means footwear made or materially adapted to individual measurements, design, materials or requirements. "Specification" means the written description, drawings, approved sample, measurements, materials and variations accepted for the order.

3. Description and Natural Variation

3.1 ModeInfinity will take reasonable care to supply footwear corresponding to the agreed description and specification.

3.2 Handmade processes and natural leather may cause non-material variations in grain, shade, texture or finish. Material variations that could reasonably affect the decision to buy must be disclosed and approved where required.

3.3 Images, sketches and digital mock-ups are illustrative unless expressly made part of the binding specification. This does not excuse misleading descriptions or material failure to match the agreed specification.

3.4 A material substitution or design change requires recorded customer approval before implementation, except for a limited action reasonably necessary to protect safety or property when the customer cannot reasonably be contacted; any such action must be explained promptly.

4. Measurements and Approval

4.1 For a custom commission, the parties must record the sizing method, relevant measurements, design, materials, finish, intended use disclosed by the customer, price and estimated timeline.

4.2 ModeInfinity will take reasonable care in measurements it undertakes and explain any material limitations of the method.

4.3 The customer must provide accurate information, attend agreed fittings where reasonably necessary, disclose fit preferences and review the final specification.

4.4 Approval confirms the specific design/measurements shown in the approval record. It does not automatically waive claims concerning negligent measurement, defective materials, workmanship, nonconformity, misrepresentation or statutory rights.

4.5 If customer-supplied measurements are used, that fact and any material limitation should be recorded. This does not automatically absolve ModeInfinity of its own duties.

4.6 Silence is not approval of a material change. Record approval by signature, email, message or another auditable method.

5. Price, Deposit and Payment

5.1 Total price, applicable taxes, delivery charges, payment milestones and methods must be stated before acceptance.

5.2 A deposit is an advance payment toward the order and is not automatically forfeited in every cancellation or dispute.

5.3 Additional charges must be stated in the accepted order, separately agreed, or otherwise lawfully payable.

5.4 ModeInfinity will issue a receipt or appropriate transaction record for each payment.

5.5 Where an agreed payment is overdue, ModeInfinity may pause unstarted work after reasonable notice where lawful and proportionate. Any storage, rescheduling or re-delivery charge must have a disclosed and lawful basis.

6. Changes

6.1 A post-approval change must be documented and state the change, price adjustment, materials already purchased/committed, effect on timing and customer approval.

6.2 ModeInfinity will not proceed with a material change before approval except as narrowly described in clause 3.4.

6.3 If the parties cannot agree, the existing approved specification remains in force unless lawfully cancelled or varied by agreement.

7. Production and Timing

7.1 Estimated start and completion dates must be stated. An estimate is not a guaranteed date unless expressly identified as a firm commitment.

7.2 ModeInfinity will use reasonable care and skill, appropriate materials and competent workmanship.

7.3 If a material delay becomes apparent, ModeInfinity will provide a reasonable update, explain the known cause and revised estimate, and discuss available options.

7.4 Customer-requested changes, missed fittings or inaccurate customer-supplied information may affect timing to the extent reasonably evidenced and communicated. They do not automatically remove statutory rights.

8. Delivery, Collection and Inspection

8.1 The order schedule states the delivery or collection method and any fitting appointments.

8.2 The customer must provide accurate delivery details and reasonably cooperate with arrangements.

8.3 Any failed-delivery, storage or re-delivery charge must be disclosed where practicable, reasonable, justified and lawful.

8.4 The customer is encouraged to inspect the order on receipt and promptly report visible damage, incorrect items or apparent non-conformity with the order reference and relevant details. This aids investigation but does not automatically extinguish statutory rights.

9. Defects, Fit Concerns and Remedies

9.1 A customer who identifies a defect, specification mismatch or fit concern should contact ModeInfinity as soon as reasonably practicable. Photos may assist but should not be demanded where unreasonable or impossible.

9.2 ModeInfinity will assess the concern fairly, considering the specification, product condition, materials, production records, wear, care, alterations and applicable law.

9.3 Depending on the facts and law, a remedy may include inspection, adjustment, repair, replacement, refund or another lawful solution. No remedy is excluded in advance where the law determines entitlement.

9.4 Where reasonably possible, contact ModeInfinity before making material alterations or commissioning third-party repairs that could prevent assessment. This does not prevent a lawful remedy where urgent action is necessary or the law provides otherwise.

9.5 Ordinary wear, misuse, failure to follow reasonable care instructions or unauthorized alterations may be relevant to causation, but do not automatically defeat a claim about an existing defect or breach.

10. Cancellation and Refund Accounting

10.1 Cancellation consequences depend on order type, stage of performance, reason, representations and applicable law. There is no blanket rule that every custom order is non-refundable.

10.2 Submit cancellation in writing. ModeInfinity should acknowledge receipt and record the production stage.

10.3 Where a deduction is lawful for cancellation not caused by ModeInfinity's breach, it should reflect documented, reasonable, order-specific costs actually incurred or irrevocably committed, less costs avoided, recoverable/reusable materials and credits required by law.

10.4 A deduction must not be a penalty, double recovery or automatic forfeiture unrelated to reasonable cost or loss. Unperformed work, speculative future profit and general overhead are not automatically chargeable.

10.5 ModeInfinity should provide an itemized statement showing payments, work completed, materials committed, supporting basis for deductions, credits and refund/balance. Supplier-confidential information may be redacted only where the calculation remains intelligible and fair.

10.6 If cancellation or non-performance is caused by ModeInfinity's breach, or the law requires a refund or other remedy, this clause does not reduce the customer's entitlement.

11. Care and Maintenance

ModeInfinity will provide care instructions reasonably relevant to the materials and construction. Customers should follow reasonable guidance on cleaning, storage, moisture, heat and maintenance. Failure to follow care instructions may be relevant where it caused the issue, but no instruction or disclaimer may disguise a defect or remove a non-excludable remedy.

12. Customer Materials and Intellectual Property

12.1 Each party retains rights it lawfully owns unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

12.2 The customer permits ModeInfinity to use supplied designs, logos, measurements and references only as reasonably necessary to quote for, make, fit, deliver and support the order.

12.3 The customer confirms, to the best of their knowledge, that they are authorized to supply those materials. They are not required to guarantee facts outside their reasonable knowledge.

12.4 ModeInfinity will not use confidential customer designs or logos for unrelated publicity without an appropriate lawful basis and permission where required.

12.5 Payment for physical footwear does not itself transfer ModeInfinity's underlying patterns, methods, templates or other IP. Any bespoke design ownership/licence must be stated expressly in the Order Schedule.

13. Personal Data

Measurements, contact details, design files, payment status and delivery information are handled under the ModeInfinity Privacy Policy and applicable law. Access must be limited to people and providers who need the information for an authorized purpose.

The parties may attempt written resolution, but this does not prevent access to a competent regulator, tribunal or court. Nigerian law applies subject to mandatory applicable law.

15. Entire Agreement and Acceptance

The Order Schedule, accepted quotation, approved specification, measurement record, payment schedule, recorded variations and these Terms form the agreement. Variations must be recorded and accepted by both parties where required. If a provision is unenforceable, the remainder applies only to the extent lawful.

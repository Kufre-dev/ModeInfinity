6. Lawful Basis for Processing

Where required by applicable data protection law, we will identify and document an appropriate lawful basis for each processing activity. Depending on the circumstances, that basis may include:

  • Consent: where you have freely agreed to a specific use and the law requires or permits consent as the basis.
  • Contract or pre-contractual steps: where processing is necessary to respond to your request, take steps toward an agreement, or perform an agreement with you.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is required to comply with a legal duty.
  • Legitimate interests: where a legitimate interest applies, the processing is necessary, and your rights and interests do not override it.
  • Other lawful bases: where specifically applicable under the governing law and circumstances.

The correct basis depends on the purpose and context. We will not rely on consent as a blanket justification for every processing activity. Where consent is used, it should be specific, informed, and capable of being withdrawn as applicable.

If we need information to take steps requested by you or fulfil an agreement, we will explain when the information is necessary and what may happen if you do not provide it.

The ModeInfinity contact workflow has been configured in the development material to use Formspree as a form-submission service. The current configuration references a Formspree endpoint, but its live status, account settings, recipients, retention arrangements, and security configuration must be verified before publication.

Depending on the form you complete, information may include your name, email address, enquiry type, message, optional telephone number, programme interest, order reference, custom design preferences, size or fit notes, and reference images or links.

If a form fails, please use the verified contact method published on the approved website. Avoid resubmitting sensitive details repeatedly or sending unnecessary personal information.

8. Shopping Cart and WhatsApp Checkout

8.1 Browser-Based Cart

The website's cart implementation uses browser local storage under the key mi_cart_v1. The described implementation stores cart item details such as product names, prices, images, and quantities on the device or browser being used.

This allows cart contents to persist between visits in the same browser until they are removed, browser storage is cleared, or another event changes the stored data. The described implementation does not include an automatic expiry period.

Local storage is not the same as a server-side account or a completed order. People with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access its stored information. Avoid using a shared device for private transactions unless you can protect your session.

8.2 WhatsApp Checkout

The described checkout workflow prepares an order message and opens WhatsApp. You must review and send the message yourself. Opening WhatsApp does not, by itself, submit an order to ModeInfinity, confirm availability, confirm payment, or guarantee acceptance of the order.

If you choose to send the message, the information you include will be processed by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy practices. Depending on the message, this may include product choices, quantities, prices, and other details you choose to provide.

Before publishing this policy, ModeInfinity must verify the WhatsApp destination number, the exact message contents, and whether any additional order-processing system receives or retains the message. No unverified telephone number should be published as an official contact.

Payment is not processed by the described cart code itself. Any payment arrangement must be separately communicated and handled through the actual approved business process.

14. Information Security

ModeInfinity should implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, disclosure, and destruction.

Measures should be proportionate to the risks and may include:

  • Restricting access to business systems and records according to role.
  • Using strong authentication and protecting account credentials.
  • Keeping website software and dependencies appropriately maintained.
  • Using secure connections and reviewing form and hosting configurations.
  • Limiting the personal information collected and shared.
  • Providing staff with privacy and security instructions.
  • Maintaining suitable backup, incident-response, and recovery arrangements.
  • Assessing third-party providers and their security arrangements.

No method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, this does not remove our responsibility to implement appropriate safeguards and respond to incidents in accordance with applicable requirements.

If a personal data breach occurs, ModeInfinity must assess and handle it under its incident-response procedure and comply with applicable notification and reporting obligations, including any relevant requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

15. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the applicable law, the nature of the processing, and any lawful limitations, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion or erasure in circumstances where the law provides that right.
  • Request restriction of processing in circumstances where it applies.
  • Object to certain processing, including relevant direct marketing.
  • Request portability of certain information where the legal conditions are met.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

These rights are not absolute in every circumstance. The applicable legal conditions, exemptions, and balancing tests may affect whether a request can be fulfilled.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the verified details in Section 19. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Please provide enough information for us to locate the relevant records, but do not send unnecessary identity documents or sensitive information.

We will handle requests within the time limits and according to the procedures required by applicable law. If we cannot fully comply, we will explain the outcome and any available next steps where required.

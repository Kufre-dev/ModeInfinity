About ModeInfinity

Where craft becomes identity.

ModeInfinity is a Nigerian footwear brand and shoemaking academy for those who value quality craftsmanship and those eager to master it. Founded in 2016, we create handcrafted footwear and provide practical training for aspiring shoemakers and entrepreneurs ready to turn their skills into a craft and a business.

Origin

From one workbench to a working craft.

ModeInfinity was birthed in 2016 by Jennifer I. Omomia, starting humbly under a tree in my father’s compound with just myself and my gateman. Named in honor of my late father, the brand emerged during a period of uncertainty when, as a young graduate, I was terrified about my future and the prospect of not finding a job. Feeling like my world was spinning out of control, my father urged me to pause and think critically about what I truly wanted.

There is always an alternative. There is always a way. It just takes time, patience, consistency, and the determination to see it through.

Deep down, I knew I was not meant for the conventional corporate rat race; I wanted to build something that brought me genuine joy. Drawing on the shoemaking skills I had acquired while studying in Ghana, I made a defining choice at that moment: to become the very best female shoemaker from this part of the world. Today, that vision has grown into a team of five, supported by a showroom at Tejuosho Shopping Complex and a workshop at the Mushin leather market.

How ModeInfinity Grew

  1. 2015

    Where It Began

    Our story began at the workbench, where an apprentice learned shoemaking through practice, patience, and precision. Those early lessons laid the foundation for the standards that guide our craft today.

  2. 2016

    Birth of a Vision

    ModeInfinity was founded on a commitment to quality craftsmanship and a craft worth passing on. That vision grew into a Nigerian footwear brand built on skilled making, practical learning, and the ambition to develop future shoemakers.

  3. 2016–2024

    The Working Years

    Through years of making footwear and serving customers, we refined our craft, strengthened our standards, and earned trust one pair at a time. The workshop became the foundation for everything ModeInfinity would build next.

  4. 2025

    Building the Next Generation

    The launch of ModeInfinity Academy extended our craft beyond the workshop. Through hands-on training with real materials and professional tools, we help students develop the skills and discipline to create footwear and carry the craft forward.

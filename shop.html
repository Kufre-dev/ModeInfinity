Size, fit & care

Three things worth knowing before you enquire.

  • How to measure

    Take a soft tape measure. Measure both feet — heel to longest toe (standing) and across the widest part of the ball. Use the longer foot. Send us the numbers in your enquiry and we'll confirm the fit.

  • Sizing

    Ready-to-wear pairs are available in sizes 38–46. For sizes outside this range, or for a made-to-measure commission, add a note in your enquiry — every ModeInfinity pair can be made to your measurements.

  • Leather care

    Wipe after wearing with a soft dry cloth. Condition every 2–3 months with a quality leather cream. Avoid prolonged water and heat exposure. Rotate pairs so the leather recovers between wears.

Uncertain about a specific leather or finish? Ask us before you enquire.

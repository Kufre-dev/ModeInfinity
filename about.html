About ModeInfinity

Where craft becomes identity.

ModeInfinity is a Nigerian workshop and academy — a place where footwear is made by hand and where the craft is taught to those who want to build with their own hands. Founded in 2016, we bring together handcrafted footwear, practical education, and purposeful design under one roof.

Origin

From one workbench to a working craft.

We do not follow trends. We build footwear meant to outlast them — made slowly, by hand, by people who care about what they are making.

ModeInfinity was founded in Abuja in 2016 by Jennifer I. Omomia. What began as one maker's practice — a workbench, a set of hand tools, and a discipline about how footwear should be made — has grown into two connected things: a footwear atelier and an academy.

Today, footwear is handcrafted in our Abuja workshop, offered from our Lagos showroom, and taught through the academy to those who want to learn the craft properly — from pattern to finish.

How ModeInfinity grew.

  1. 2015

    Where It Began

    Started as an apprentice, learning the fundamentals of hand shoemaking. Every pair began on a single workbench; every mistake was corrected by hand. Those first years were about learning why each step matters, not about producing quickly.

  2. 2016

    Birth of a Vision

    Founded ModeInfinity — not first as a brand, and not yet as a school, but as a vision for what shoemaking could be if it were practiced properly and passed on. That idea shaped everything that followed: the workshop, the showroom, and eventually the academy. The business grew from it. It did not come first.

  3. 2016–2024

    The Working Years

    For nearly a decade, the practice was straightforward: make shoes properly, sell them honestly, and refine the standard with every pair. The bench grew from one maker's practice into a workshop with returning clients and a reputation built pair by pair — not by marketing.

  4. 2025

    Legacy Building

    Launched the ModeInfinity Academy — the moment the work stopped being only about making footwear and started being about passing the craft on. Students learn by making, from first cut to final finish, on real material with real tools. What the workshop spent nine years building, the Academy was created to hand forward.

What We Stand For

The principles behind everything we make and everything we teach.

Our Mission

To make footwear properly — by hand, at a standard worth keeping — and to teach that standard to anyone willing to learn it. We train artisans to work with discipline, judgement, and pride in what leaves their bench. Success, for us, is measured by what our graduates can build on their own — not by what we say about them.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where handcrafted footwear is treated as serious work, practised at an international standard, and passed on to people who will keep the craft alive. Not a market flooded with shoes — a craft with a future.

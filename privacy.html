Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 2026

ModeInfinity (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

We operate in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, its General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025, and align our practices with international standards such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for visitors outside Nigeria.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Submit an enquiry: Name, email address, phone number (optional), enquiry type, program interest, custom order details, and message content.
  • Place an order (via WhatsApp): Name, delivery address, phone number, shoe size, and payment confirmation details.
  • Subscribe to our newsletter: Email address.

We also automatically collect certain technical data when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, and interaction data (via cookies or analytics tools, where applicable).

2. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the NDPA and GDPR, we must have a lawful basis for processing your personal data. We rely on the following:

  • Consent: When you submit our contact form or opt-in to marketing, you give explicit consent for us to use your data to respond to your enquiry.
  • Contract: When you commission a pair of shoes or enrol in the Academy, we process your data to fulfil our contractual obligations.
  • Legitimate Interest: To improve our website, secure our services, and prevent fraud.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with Nigerian tax, accounting, or consumer protection laws.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries, custom order requests, and Academy applications.
  • Process and fulfil orders, including communicating about lead times and delivery.
  • Send administrative information, such as updates to our terms or policies.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

4. Data Sharing and International Transfers

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate:

  • Formspree: Our contact form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io), which stores data on servers located outside Nigeria. Formspree is GDPR-compliant and processes data under strict data processing agreements.
  • Google Fonts: Our website loads fonts from Google, which may collect your IP address for technical delivery.
  • WhatsApp: If you choose to checkout via WhatsApp, your data is processed by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) under their privacy policy.

Where we transfer data outside Nigeria, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, in line with the NDPA’s cross-border transfer rules.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Enquiry data is typically retained for 24 months. Order data is retained for 7 years to comply with Nigerian financial record-keeping laws.

6. Your Rights

Under the NDPA 2023 (and the GDPR for international visitors), you have the right to:

  • Be Informed: Know how your data is collected and used.
  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data.
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at officialmodeinfinity11@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) within 72 hours and inform you without undue delay, in accordance with NDPA requirements.

8. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to function properly. We do not use invasive tracking cookies or advertising cookies without your explicit consent. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

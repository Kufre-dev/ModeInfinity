FAQ

Quick answers.

The questions we hear most — about the Academy, custom orders, and visiting the workshop.

Academy

How do I apply to the Academy?

Choose "Academy application" in the form above and tell us which program interests you. We'll arrange a short consultation and a workshop tour before you secure a place in the next cohort.

Academy

How much do the academy programs cost?

Fees vary by pathway and duration. Payment plans are available. Enquire with your chosen program and we'll send a full breakdown, including what's covered.

Academy

Do I need prior shoemaking experience?

No. The Foundation Craft program is designed for beginners with little to no workshop experience. We start from the absolute basics.

Academy

Are tools and materials provided?

Yes. We provide professional-grade tools, industrial machines, and premium leather for all practical work during the program. You do not need to purchase anything before you arrive.

Academy

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Upon successful completion of the program and assessment of your final work, you'll receive a ModeInfinity Certificate of Completion.

Orders

How long does a custom pair take?

Most commissions are completed in 3–4 weeks from the date measurements, leather, and deposit are confirmed. Complex designs may extend this — we confirm your timeline in writing before you commit.

Orders

Can I get a pair in my size or colour if it isn't listed?

Yes. Select "Custom order" in the form and send your size, preferred style, and colour. We'll confirm what's possible and quote you.

Orders

What's the difference between ready-to-wear and custom?

Ready-to-wear pairs are made from our standard sizing (38–46) and available from the shop. Custom pairs are made to your exact measurements, leather, and finish — enquire via this form.

Orders

What if my custom pair doesn't fit perfectly?

We offer fitting adjustments within the first 30 days of delivery. Contact us with photos and we'll arrange a secure return to the workshop where the pair is adjusted to match your measurements.

Orders

Do you offer repairs and restoration?

Yes — we repair ModeInfinity pairs made in our workshop. Contact us with photos and the approximate date of your commission, and we'll quote repair costs before any work begins.

Visit

Can I visit the workshop before committing?

Yes — book a private tour of the Abuja workshop. Tours are by appointment (Mon–Fri), last about 30 minutes, and carry no obligation. Maximum group size: 5.

Visit

Do you ship internationally?

We ship nationwide across Nigeria. International shipping is available on request — include your destination and we'll confirm timing and cost.

Visit

What are your business hours?

The Abuja workshop and Lagos showroom are open Monday to Friday, 9:00–18:00. Saturday visits are available by appointment. Closed Sundays.

Business

Do you offer partnerships or corporate training?

Yes. We work with organisations on group training, youth skills programmes, and brand collaborations. Contact us with "Partnership enquiry" selected and a short note on what you have in mind.

Business

Can I launch a business after graduation?

The Enterprise & Brand pathway is structured for exactly that — graduates leave with the technical skills and business foundations to launch their own practice. We also offer post-programme support through our alumni network.

Business

How do you source your materials?

We source leather and components from trusted Nigerian and international suppliers, selected for grain, finish, and durability. Every batch is checked before it enters a pair.

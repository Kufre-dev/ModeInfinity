The Academy

Learn the craft of footwear. Build your skill.

Explore three pathways from beginner skills to professional shoemaking and building your own footwear brand. Gain hands-on experience in the atelier, master your craft, and turn your skills into a profession. Contact the Academy for programme details, fees, and upcoming intakes.

Learning pathways

01 3 Months

Start here

Foundations

A hands-on introduction to shoemaking, covering essential tools, materials, pattern making, construction, and finishing. Build your foundation by making your first pair from the ground up.

Explore programme
02 6 Months

Develop

Professional

Develop stronger technical ability through deeper hands-on practice, refined construction, professional finishing, and the discipline required to produce footwear to a consistent standard.

Explore programme
03 1–3 Years

Build

Entrepreneur

Take your craftsmanship into business. Develop the technical, creative, and commercial skills to produce independently, price your work, build your brand, and grow your own footwear line.
